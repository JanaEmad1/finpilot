Common reasons a card payment is declined:
1. The card is **frozen** or not yet activated.
2. There is **not enough money** in the account in that currency and auto-exchange is off.
3. A **security setting** blocks it — for example online, contactless, magstripe or ATM
   payments are switched off under **Cards → Security**.
4. The payment looked unusual and was stopped by fraud checks; you will get a notification
   asking you to confirm it was you.

**Contactless:** for your security, after several contactless payments in a row the terminal
may ask for chip-and-PIN once. Contactless limits depend on the country.

**Where can I use my card?** Anywhere Visa or Mastercard is accepted (depending on your card).

**Apple Pay / Google Pay:** add your card from the card screen in the app with **Add to wallet**.
