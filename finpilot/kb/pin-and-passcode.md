---
title: PIN, blocked PIN and app passcode
intents: change_pin, pin_blocked, passcode_forgotten
---
You can **view your PIN** in the app under **Cards → Show PIN** after confirming your identity.

**Changing your PIN:** in many countries you can change it at any ATM that offers "PIN change".

**PIN blocked** after 3 wrong attempts: unblock it in the app under **Cards → Security →
Unblock PIN**, then use it once at an ATM or chip-and-PIN terminal.

**Forgot your app passcode:** on the login screen tap **Forgot passcode**. We send a code to
your email and may ask for a selfie to confirm it is you.
