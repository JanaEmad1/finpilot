---
title: Managing your account and personal details
intents: edit_personal_details, terminate_account, age_limit, country_support
---
**Personal details:** change your email, phone number or address under **Profile → Personal
details**. Changing your legal name needs a new ID document.

**Closing your account:** move or withdraw your money first, then go to **Profile → Close
account**. Closing is free.

**Age:** you must be **18 or older** to open a standard account. Teenagers aged 13–17 can have a
junior account managed by a parent.

**Countries:** you can open an account if you legally live in the EEA, the UK, or one of our
other supported countries (listed in the app during sign-up). You can use your card worldwide.
