intents: supported_cards_and_currencies, visa_or_mastercard

Your FinPilot card is issued as **Visa or Mastercard** depending on your country and card type;
you can see which one on the card itself and in the app.

For **topping up**, we accept Visa, Mastercard and Maestro debit and credit cards. American
Express is not supported.

You can hold, exchange and spend **over 25 currencies**, including EUR, GBP, USD, CHF, PLN, JPY
and AED. Crypto-currencies are not part of your current account.
