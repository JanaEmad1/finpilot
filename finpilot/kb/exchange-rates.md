You can hold and exchange **over 25 currencies** in the app under **Exchange**. On weekdays
we use the interbank rate. On **weekends** a small markup (1%) is added because markets are
closed.

**Monthly exchange allowance:** the first 1,000 EUR per month is exchanged without a fee;
above that a 0.5% fee applies.

**The rate on my payment looks wrong:** card payments in a foreign currency are converted at
the rate when the payment is **completed**, which can be a few days after you paid. If the
shop or ATM offered to charge you in your home currency ("dynamic currency conversion"),
they chose the rate — always choose to pay in the **local currency** to get our rate.
