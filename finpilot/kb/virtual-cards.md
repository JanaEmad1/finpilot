---
title: Virtual and disposable cards
intents: get_disposable_virtual_card, getting_virtual_card, disposable_card_limits, virtual_card_not_working
---
A **virtual card** works like your physical card for online payments and Apple Pay / Google Pay,
and it is created instantly in the app under **Cards → Add → Virtual card**.

A **disposable virtual card** gets new card details after every payment, which protects you
on websites you don't fully trust. You can create up to 5 disposable cards per day.
Disposable cards cannot be used for recurring payments (subscriptions), and refunds to a
disposable card go back to your account balance instead.

**If a virtual card is declined:** check that it is not frozen, that you typed the details
and billing address correctly, and that the merchant accepts virtual cards. Some hotels,
car rental companies and petrol pumps need a physical card.
