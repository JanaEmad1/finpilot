---
title: Lost, stolen or compromised card or phone
intents: lost_or_stolen_card, compromised_card, lost_or_stolen_phone, card_swallowed
---
**Act fast: freeze your card first.** In the app go to **Cards → Freeze**, or ask this
assistant to freeze it. Freezing is instant and can be undone at any time.

If you are sure the card is gone or someone else used the details, **terminate** it and
order a replacement — a new card with a new number is sent free of charge.

If you see payments you did not make, report them under **Transaction → Report a problem**.
We review them and, for unauthorised payments, refund you within 10 working days.

**Lost phone:** log in from another device or the website and log out your old phone under
**Security → Devices**, then change your passcode. Your cards keep working, but you may want
to freeze them until you have your phone back.

**Card kept by an ATM:** freeze the card, then contact the bank that owns the ATM. If you
cannot get it back the same day, order a replacement.
