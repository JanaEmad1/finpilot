---
title: Verifying your identity and source of funds
intents: verify_my_identity, unable_to_verify_identity, why_verify_identity, verify_source_of_funds
---
**Why do you need to verify me?** Banking laws require us to confirm who our customers are,
to prevent fraud and money laundering.

**How:** in the app, take a photo of an ID document (passport, national ID or driving licence)
and a short selfie video. Most checks finish in minutes.

**Verification failed?** Make sure the photo is sharp, not cropped, without glare, and that the
document is valid. You can try again from **Profile → Verify identity**.

**Source of funds:** sometimes we must ask where money came from — for example a payslip,
a property sale contract, or a bank statement. Upload it in the app when asked.
