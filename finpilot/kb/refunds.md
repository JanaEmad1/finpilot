---
title: Refunds
intents: request_refund, Refund_not_showing_up
---
Refunds for card payments must be requested from the **merchant** first. Once the merchant
issues a refund, it normally reaches your account in **3–5 working days**, but some merchants
take up to 15 days.

If the merchant confirmed a refund more than 15 days ago and it still doesn't show:
open the original transaction and tap **Report a problem → Refund not received**. Upload
the merchant's refund confirmation if you have it.

If the merchant refuses a refund you are entitled to (for example, goods never arrived),
you can open a **chargeback dispute** from the transaction.
