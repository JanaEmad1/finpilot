---
title: My top-up is pending, failed or was reverted
intents: pending_top_up, top_up_failed, top_up_reverted, balance_not_updated_after_cheque_or_cash_deposit
---
- **Card top-ups** normally arrive instantly. If one is **pending**, it may be under a
  security check; this usually finishes within a few hours.
- **Failed top-up:** check that the card is in your name, not expired, and that 3-D Secure
  was completed. Your card issuer may also have blocked it — contact them.
- **Reverted top-up:** if we could not verify the payment, the money is returned to the card
  you used. It can take 3–5 working days to appear on that card's statement.
- **Bank transfer top-ups** can take up to 1 working day (up to 5 for international ones).
- **Cash or cheque deposits** made at another bank and sent on to us arrive like a bank
  transfer; allow up to 5 working days before contacting us.
