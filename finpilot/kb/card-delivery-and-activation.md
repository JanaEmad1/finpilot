You can order a physical card in the app under **Cards → Order card**. Standard delivery takes
**3–7 working days** in most countries; express delivery (1–3 days) is available for a fee.
You can follow the delivery status in the app.

If your card has not arrived after 10 working days, open the card in the app and tap
**Card not arrived** — we will cancel it and send a new one for free.

**Activating:** when the card arrives, open **Cards**, tap **Activate** and enter the
6-digit code printed on the letter, or make a first chip-and-PIN payment.

**Spare cards:** you can hold up to 3 physical cards on one account; extra cards have the
same fee as the first one.

**Linking a card:** a card you already hold can be linked to the app by scanning it or by
entering the last 4 digits and the expiry date.

**Expiring cards:** we send a replacement automatically about 30 days before your card
expires, as long as your address is up to date.
