**How long does a transfer take?** Transfers between FinPilot users are instant. Local bank
transfers usually arrive the same day; international (SWIFT) transfers take **1–5 working days**.

**Cancelling:** you can cancel a transfer while it is still **pending** from the transaction
screen. Once it is completed it cannot be cancelled — ask the recipient to send it back.

**Transfer not received:** check the recipient's details on the transaction and share the
transfer confirmation (from the transaction screen) with them — their bank can trace it.

**Declined or failed transfer:** common reasons are wrong account details, the recipient's bank
rejecting it, missing information for security checks, or a **beneficiary not allowed** because
of sanctions or our risk rules. Failed transfers are refunded to your balance automatically.
