---
title: Receiving money into your account
intents: receiving_money, transfer_into_account, balance_not_updated_after_bank_transfer
---
Find your **account details** (IBAN and BIC, or account number and sort code) in the app under
**Accounts → Details**. Share them with anyone who wants to pay you.

Incoming local transfers usually arrive within minutes to a few hours; international transfers
can take up to 5 working days.

**My balance hasn't updated after a transfer:** ask the sender for the transfer reference and
confirm they used the details shown in your app exactly. If more than 5 working days have passed,
contact us with the sender's proof of payment.
