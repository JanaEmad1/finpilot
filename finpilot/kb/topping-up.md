You can add money by **bank transfer** (using your account details in the app), by **debit or
credit card**, or with **Apple Pay / Google Pay**. We do not accept cash or cheques directly.

**Auto top-up** adds money automatically when your balance drops below an amount you choose.
Turn it on under **Accounts → Top up → Auto top-up**.

**Limits:** card top-ups are limited to 10,000 EUR per 30 days for standard accounts.

**Verifying a card top-up:** for security we may ask you to verify the card you top up with by
entering the amount of a small temporary charge or by uploading a photo of the card.
