---
title: A payment I don't recognise or was charged twice
intents: card_payment_not_recognised, direct_debit_payment_not_recognised, cash_withdrawal_not_recognised, transaction_charged_twice, extra_charge_on_statement
---
Before reporting a payment, check:
- **The merchant name** — companies often use a different name on statements than their
  shop name. Tap the transaction to see the merchant details and location.
- **Other people using your card** or subscriptions you forgot about.
- **Pending vs. final** — a hotel or rental may place a temporary hold and a final charge,
  which can look like a double charge. The hold usually disappears within 7 days.

If you still don't recognise it: **freeze your card** and report the payment under
**Transaction → Report a problem**. For direct debits you did not authorise, you can ask for
a refund under the direct debit guarantee.

A real **double charge** is usually refunded automatically by the merchant within 5 working
days; if not, raise a dispute from the transaction.
