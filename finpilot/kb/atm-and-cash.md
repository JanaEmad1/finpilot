---
title: ATMs and cash withdrawals
intents: atm_support, declined_cash_withdrawal, wrong_amount_of_cash_received, cash_withdrawal_charge
---
You can withdraw cash at any ATM showing the Visa or Mastercard logo.

**Fees:** each month you get free withdrawals up to **200 EUR** (or 5 withdrawals, whichever
comes first). After that we charge **2%** of the amount (minimum 1 EUR). The ATM owner may
add its own fee, which is shown on the ATM screen.

**Declined withdrawal:** check your balance, your ATM security setting, and whether you
entered the right PIN. Some ATMs don't accept foreign cards.

**Wrong amount of cash:** if the ATM gave you less cash than it charged, report it from the
transaction in the app within 60 days. We contact the ATM owner and usually resolve it
within 30 days.
