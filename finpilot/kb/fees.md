---
title: Fees and charges
intents: card_payment_fee_charged, transfer_fee_charged, top_up_by_bank_transfer_charge, top_up_by_card_charge
---
- **Card payments:** free in any currency, except the weekend exchange markup (see exchange rates).
- **Transfers:** transfers in the same currency to local bank accounts are free. International
  transfers have a fee that is shown **before** you confirm the transfer.
- **Top up by bank transfer:** free.
- **Top up by card:** free for cards issued in the EU/UK; cards from other regions and
  business cards have a fee of up to 2.5%, shown before you confirm.

If you see a fee you didn't expect, tap the transaction to see the fee breakdown.
