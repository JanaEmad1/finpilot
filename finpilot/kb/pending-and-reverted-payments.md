---
title: Pending and reverted payments
intents: pending_card_payment, reverted_card_payment?, pending_cash_withdrawal
---
When you pay by card, the merchant first **reserves** the money. The payment shows as
**pending** until the merchant completes it, usually within **1–3 working days** (up to 7 for
some merchants such as hotels and car rentals).

If the merchant never completes the payment, the reserved money is **reverted** — returned
to your balance automatically. A reverted payment means you were not charged.

Sometimes a payment is reverted and then charged again a few days later; this is normal
when the merchant completes it late. Cash withdrawals follow the same pending process.
